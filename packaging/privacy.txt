jobdork and your privacy

jobdork runs on this computer and keeps what you give it here: your
settings, the job posts it finds, your resume, and the letters you ask it
to write, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\jobdork and Documents\job-applications. Nobody
who makes jobdork receives any of it. There are no accounts, no analytics,
no advertising and no tracking.

It goes on the network to do what you set it up to do:

  - Searching job boards and employers' careers sites, with your job titles
    and place as search terms, when you scan (or at the times you choose
    under Settings, "Schedule a run").
  - Sending your resume and job adverts to an AI provider, only if you
    choose one on the AI page (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Groq, under their own
    privacy policies; or Ollama, on a computer you choose).
  - Sending a report to the people who make jobdork, only when you press
    Send on the Feedback page. You see exactly what is sent first.

And one thing by itself:

  - Once a day, when the Dashboard opens, it asks GitHub whether a newer
    jobdork is out (one request to api.github.com; nothing about you is
    sent). To turn this off, press "Turn off the daily check" under
    Settings, Updates.

When you press Update, it downloads the new installer from GitHub and,
before opening it, asks GitHub (api.github.com) and Sigstore
(tuf-repo-cdn.sigstore.dev) for what it needs to check that jobdork's own
release workflow built it. Nothing about you is sent.

When a scan finds new job posts, or a newer jobdork is out, Windows shows
a notification from jobdork, with its sound. Settings, Notifications turns
them off.

Uninstalling removes the program and leaves your data in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\jobdork, so a reinstall finds it. Delete that folder, or use
"Start over" on the Dashboard first, to remove everything.
