AI reader

A model reads the full advert against your résumé and gives its own verdict beside the match score, and can read posting pages that do not say plainly whether the job is still open. It never hides a job post. Local models cost nothing; the others spend tokens on your key.

Kept only in this server's memory and never written to the config, the database, a log, or sent back to this page. Forgotten when you stop jobdork, when you click Forget, or a minute after the last dashboard tab closes.
Judging needs a résumé and at least 200 characters of advert. A 26B local model takes about a minute per job post, and the check adds two more calls to each.
