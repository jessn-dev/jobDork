AI reader

A model reads the full advert against your resume and gives its own verdict beside the match score, and can read posting pages that do not say plainly whether the job is still open. It never hides a job post. Local models cost nothing; the others spend tokens on your key.

Kept only in this server's memory and never written to the config, the database, a log, or sent back to this page. Forgotten when you stop jobdork, when you click Forget, or a minute after the last dashboard tab closes.
Judging needs a resume and at least 200 characters of advert. A 26B local model takes about a minute per job post, and the check adds two more calls to each.

Dealbreakers

A pattern read against the advert, not the title; case is ignored. One that hides the job post drops it; one that costs points keeps it, 8 points lower, with a warning. The pattern is a regular expression: security clearance|TS/SCI matches either.

How to write a pattern, with samples

A pattern is a regular expression: plain words, plus a few symbols for "or", "maybe" and "any number". Plain words alone work: polygraph finds "Polygraph" anywhere in the advert. Capitals never matter.

WriteMeansExampleFindsDoes not find
|OrTS/SCI|top secret"TS/SCI", "Top Secret""Secret Santa"
.Any one characteron.call"on-call", "on call""oncall"
?The thing before it is optionalon.?call"on-call", "on call", "oncall""on the call"
\bEdge of a word\bC2C\b"C2C only""B2C2C"
\dAny digit\d\d%"50%""5%"
{2}Exactly that many of the thing before\d{2}%"25%""5%"
(?:a|b)A group, usually to put an "or" inside wordsunpaid (?:trial|project)"unpaid trial", "unpaid project""unpaid leave"
\s?An optional space\d{2}\s?%"25%", "25 %"

To match one of . ? | ( ) [ ] { } + * ^ $ \ itself, put \ before it: C\+\+ finds "C++".

Resume

Used to rank job posts by skill overlap, offline and free. It is also what every drafted document is built from. Without one, a CV would be inventing a career rather than tailoring one.

Running in a container: the resume and the documents below are kept in a temporary folder, emptied when jobdork stops or starts. Upload the resume again after a restart, and download a letter you want to keep.

Projects

Projects your resume does not describe well, in your own words. A tailored resume may use them, and its checks count them as part of your record. Whether they get a section at all is decided per job post: near the top for a student or a career changer, beside the jobs for a freelancer, and folded into the jobs for someone with years in the field.

Resume review

The model on the AI page reads your resume and lists what to fix, each point quoting the line it is about. A point that quotes something not in your resume is dropped. To compare it with one job post, use Review resume for this post in that post's window.
