# The user guide

jobdork finds job posts for you. It reads job boards and employers' own
career sites, checks every post against what you asked for (job titles,
place, pay, the things you will not do), scores it against your resume, and
remembers what you already did about each one. If you want, an AI reads the
adverts too, and helps you write a cover letter or tailor your resume.

Everything stays on your computer. There is no account, and jobdork never
applies for a job for you.

![The Job posts page](images/job-posts.png)

## How it works, in six steps

1. **Install it and answer setup's questions** (about five minutes):
   your resume, an AI (optional), the job titles you want, where you are,
   and your pay. [Getting started](getting-started.md)
2. **Run a scan.** jobdork reads the boards and keeps the posts that pass
   your rules. The first one finishes in about a minute.
   [The Dashboard](dashboard.md)
3. **Read your job posts**, best first. Each has a **Match** score, a
   **Fit** score and, if you set up AI, an **AI** score.
   [Job posts](job-posts.md) and [Scores explained](scores.md)
4. **Open a post** to read the whole advert, see which of your
   certifications it asks for, and use the AI tools.
   [The job post window](job-window.md)
5. **Mark what you did**: interested, applied, interviewing, and so on.
   jobdork remembers, and the next scan shows only what is new.
   [Statuses and tags](statuses.md)
6. **Let it run by itself**: a daily scan, with a notification when it
   finds something. [Settings](settings.md)

## The pages of the app

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [Dashboard](dashboard.md) | Start a scan and the other runs; see how they went |
| [Job posts](job-posts.md) | The posts that passed your rules, with filters |
| [Search](search.md) | What you are looking for: titles, places, pay, dealbreakers |
| [Resume](resume.md) | Your resume, projects and education; resume review; cover letters |
| [Sources](sources.md) | Where posts come from; adding an employer or one post |
| [AI](ai.md) | Choose the AI models that read adverts and check their work |
| [Feedback](feedback.md) | Tell the people who make jobdork about a problem or an idea |
| [Settings](settings.md) | Appearance, scheduled scans, notifications, updates, clean-up |

New words, such as *board*, *dealbreaker* or *settled*, are in the
[glossary](glossary.md).
