# Glossary

**Advert**
: The text of a job post: what the job is, what it needs, what it pays.

**AI score**
: The AI model's opinion of how well you fit a post, out of 100.
  [Scores explained](scores.md#ai-the-models-opinion)

**Applicant tracking system (ATS)**
: The software employers use to receive and sort applications. It reads
  your resume for the advert's words: a job post's **ATS match** scores how
  well yours does ([how](job-window.md#ats-match)), and the Resume page
  checks whether it can read your file at all
  ([how](resume.md#how-a-tracking-system-reads-it)).

**Application**
: A job you applied to, kept on [My applications](applications.md) with
  its stages, next step, contact, the documents you sent and your notes.

**Board**
: A place job posts are listed: a job board, or an employer's own career
  site (an *employer board*).

**Brief**
: What jobdork gives a coding agent to write a document from: the same
  instructions, advert and resume a model would get. [AI](ai.md#coding-agent)

**Checker**
: A second AI model that checks the first one's work. [AI](ai.md#checkers)

**Coding agent**
: An AI assistant you already use for code (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI,
  Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude Desktop), connected to jobdork to do its
  writing with no API key. [AI](ai.md#coding-agent)

**Copy**
: The same job posted in more than one place. Copies show as one post
  (**+1 copy**) and share one status.

**Dealbreaker**
: Words in an advert that rule a job out for you, or cost it points.
  [Search](search.md#dealbreakers)

**Dropped**
: Found by a scan but left out by your rules. See them under *Dropped by
  your filters*. [Job posts](job-posts.md#dropped-by-your-filters)

**Fit**
: How many of the advert's skills are on your resume, out of 25.
  [Scores explained](scores.md#fit-your-skills-against-the-adverts)

**Ghost listing**
: A post over 90 days old: almost never a real opening. Dropped.

**Hallucination**
: A fact an AI states that is not true. Every AI output is checked for them.
  [AI](ai.md#the-hallucination-check)

**Key (API key)**
: A password-like code from an AI company or a job source that lets jobdork
  use it on your account. Held in memory only, or kept in your computer's
  keychain if you choose ([Your keys](settings.md#your-keys)); never in a file.

**Match**
: Points for everything you asked for, added up. Not a percentage.
  [Scores explained](scores.md#match-everything-you-asked-for-added-up)

**MCP (Model Context Protocol)**
: The common way coding agents connect to a tool such as jobdork. jobdork
  starts its MCP server on your computer when your agent asks for it; it
  opens no network port. [AI](ai.md#coding-agent)

**Ollama**
: A free program that runs AI models on your own computer, so nothing
  leaves it. [ollama.com](https://ollama.com)

**Pursuing**
: The statuses applied, submitted, interviewing and offer. These posts are
  never removed by a clean-up or a rule change.

**Radius**
: How far from one of your cities a job may be.

**Reader**
: An AI model that reads adverts and writes for you. You can list up to
  six; the next answers when one cannot. [AI](ai.md#readers)

**Recruiter**
: A post by a recruitment or staffing firm for a client.
  [Statuses and tags](statuses.md#recruiter)

**Risky, stale**
: A post 8 to 21 days old (risky) or older (stale): ranked lower, still
  shown. [Age tags](statuses.md#how-old-a-post-is)

**Salary floor**
: The lowest yearly pay you would take. Only a post that *states* lower pay
  is hidden. [Search](search.md#location-work-arrangement-and-pay)

**Scan**
: One read of every source for new job posts. [Dashboard](dashboard.md)

**Settled**
: The statuses rejected, withdrawn, skipped and closed: done with, so
  hidden from the list.

**Source**
: Somewhere jobdork reads job posts from. [Sources](sources.md)

**Stage**
: Where an application is: applied, interviewing, offer or closed. Each
  status it is given is dated. [My applications](applications.md)

**Status**
: Where you are with a job: new, viewed, interested, applied and so on.
  [Statuses](statuses.md#statuses)

**Suggestion**
: A project, education entry or skill a coding agent proposed for your
  record. It counts for nothing until you approve it.
  [Resume](resume.md#suggestions)
